⚡ How to Use Know Your Campus — Step by Step
1

Visit the Website

Go to knowyourcampus.in on your phone or computer — no login, no signup needed.

2

Search or Browse Colleges

Type a college name, city, or course in the search bar. Or use filters — Government / Private, NAAC rating, city.

3

Open a College Profile

Click any college to view its full profile — courses, fees, seats, cutoffs, facilities and student reviews.

4

Compare Colleges

Select 2–3 colleges and click Compare to see them side by side. Makes your decision much easier.

5

Write a Review

Already studying? Help other students by writing an honest review and rating for your college.

6

Submit Admission Inquiry

Interested in a college? Fill the admission form — the college team will contact you directly.
