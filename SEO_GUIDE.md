## 🚀 Next Steps for Google Indexing

### Step 1: Deploy Your Website
Your website needs to be **live on the internet** with a proper domain name (e.g., knowyourcampus.com).

**Options:**
- **Vercel** (Recommended for React) - Free
- **Netlify** - Free
- **AWS** / **Google Cloud** / **Azure**

### Step 2: Submit to Google Search Console
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add your website property
3. Verify ownership (via HTML file or DNS)
4. Submit your sitemap: `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`

### Step 3: Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Add and verify your site
3. Submit sitemap

### Step 4: Create Quality Content
- Add detailed college pages
- Write blog posts about admissions
- Add student reviews
- Create comparison guides

### Step 5: Build Backlinks
- List on education directories
- Partner with colleges
- Guest posts on education blogs
- Social media presence

### Step 6: Local SEO (Optional)
- Google My Business listing
- Local directories
- Location-specific pages

## 📊 How Long Until Google Shows Your Site?

- **Initial Indexing**: 1-4 weeks after submission
- **Ranking for Keywords**: 3-6 months
- **Top Rankings**: 6-12 months (with consistent effort)
